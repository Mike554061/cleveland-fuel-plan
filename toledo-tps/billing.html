Method. Controllables move with the run; fixed costs are day-allocated. Contribution margin is net sales less controllables — the number that says whether a marginal load is worth taking. Operating profit is after fixed allocation. Driver wages are charged once, on on-duty hours, so load-out labour is not double counted.
