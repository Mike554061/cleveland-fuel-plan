How it runs

Sequence

West to south, in one pass

The schools are ordered by ZIP cluster so the truck works through them in a single direction and never doubles back. Four of the legs between buildings are under a mile.

Handling

One school, one pallet

Each building's order is built, labeled and wrapped to its own pallet and loaded in reverse order of delivery, so nothing is broken down or sorted at the door.

Accountability

Proof at every drop

Temperature-controlled the whole way, with a photographed proof of delivery captured at each school and a named operations contact for the route.
