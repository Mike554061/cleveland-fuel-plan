Pete, Shameria, Vince — the WEX fleet card is coming. Any day now; we're just waiting on the plastic. When it lands, this page becomes part of the ask.

The ask

  • No more filling up on East 55th. It is the most expensive fuel we buy, and we buy a lot of it.
  • Fill at the stops listed for your route. Every one of them is already on your path — these are not side trips, they're places you drive past anyway.

Why it matters on our end

Buying consistently at the same providers earns the company a back end on volume. That's real money coming back into this market — but it only shows up if we're consistent about where we fill.

My promise to you

  • Until the card is live, I'll give you simplified solutions so nobody is sitting at a pump sorting this out. You should not have to fight this to do your job.

Appreciate you. — Mike
